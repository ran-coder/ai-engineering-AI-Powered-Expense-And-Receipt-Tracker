| **Problem Evidence** | Manual expense tracking like logging in the notes app or sheets based on receipts and memory creates bottlenecks, especially as companies scale (SoftCo, 2025). This approach is slow and prone to human mistakes, making it critical for small businesses needing visibility of where their money goes. Without a proper and accurate tracking, people suffer with overspending and friction around financial planning.  Moreover, many users spread their money across several banks and e-wallets and pay using cash, cards, and QR codes. According to The Edge Malaysia (2022), managing expenses has become increasingly complex and inconvenient due to the fragmentation of funds across multiple e-wallets (such as Touch ’n Go, Grab, and 1Pay), physical cards, bank accounts, and emergency cash. Unlike relying on traditional cash, tracking daily spending now requires constant monitoring of various digital balances and physical card top-ups, a burden that will only grow as more retailers launch their own proprietary payment apps.  |
| **Existing Approaches** | 1\. **Pawar et al. (2025)**: Developed a financial assistant that lets users manage their finance through typed commands in natural language. It uses a PHP front end, a Python Flask back end, and a MySQL database. When a user asks “What was my coffee expenditure last month?”Google's Gemini LLM interprets the question and generates output through the SQL query. 2\. **Guru & Subramaniam (2026):** Built a personalized, real-time expense categorization system. It uses OCR to extract the merchant, date, and amount from physical and digital receipts, then uses retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to categorize each expense, drawing on the user’s past spending patterns and preferred categories. 3\. **Lin et al. (2022):** Built an automatic receipt recognition system (ARRS) that reads receipts using deep learning. It reports 80.93% accuracy on handwritten characters, and its YOLOv4-s model reached 99.39% accuracy on printed characters, with only 33 characters misjudged. |
| **Project / Engineering Gap** | Existing receipt scanners and bank apps each cover only part of a person’s spending, and the reviewed approaches do not combine receipt items, QR payments, and bank records into one categorized view with grounded, personalized insights. |
| **Objective O1** | To design an AI-powered expense and receipts tracker with personalized insights via retrieval-based question answering for Malaysia receipts.  |
| **Objective O2** | To develop a system that extracts and categorizes transaction information from user-submitted receipts, QR payment confirmations, and bank statements using open-source OCR and NLP models, and answers spending questions through an LLM with SQL queries. |
| **Objective O3 (if relevant)** | To evaluate the solution in terms of extraction and answer quality, groundedness and safety.   |
| **Intended Users** | Digitally active consumers who frequently use mobile banking and e-wallet solutions.   |
| **Supported Tasks** | Upload receipts, QR payment confirmations, and bank statements. Extract and review the transaction details (merchant, date, amount, items). Categorize transactions  Compute and visualize spending summaries by category and over time. Personalized insights and recommendations based on summary  Answer questions about spending in natural language and get grounded answers |
| **Information / Knowledge Domain** | Personal and small-business spending records in Malaysia. Include physical/digital receipts and online payment confirmations.  |
| **Operating Context** | Run on a mobile application Requires an internet connection, relies on user-submitted data and runs as a prototype for evaluation, not a production financial service.  |
| **Important Constraints** | Rely on open source OCR, NLP models and LLMs APIs No public dataset of e-wallet payment screenshots Limited by the LLMs provider’s rate limits, cost, and downtime User data would be sent to a third-party (LLMs APIs)  |
| **Explicit Out-of-Scope Tasks** | Capture transactions automatically from bank or e-wallet accounts Store sensitive identifiers  Initiate payments to any e-wallet accounts Answer question that is not in the user’s stored record  Guarantee accurate extraction for handwritten receipts   |
| **Evaluation Focus** |  Extraction accuracy Categorization accuracy Answer groundedness Usability Responsible AI risks Integration and end-to-end testing |
